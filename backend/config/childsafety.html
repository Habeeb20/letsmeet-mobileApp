Child Safety Standards

Let's Meet's public commitment against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), and how we prevent, detect, and report it.

Zero Tolerance Policy

Let's Meet has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) of any kind. Our platform is strictly for adults aged 18 and older, and any content, account, or behavior that sexualizes, exploits, endangers, or otherwise harms a minor is immediately removed and reported to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and local law enforcement where applicable.

Age Requirement

Let's Meet is an 18+ platform. Users must confirm they are at least 18 years old during account registration. Accounts found or reported to belong to a minor are suspended immediately upon detection, pending investigation.

How We Prevent CSAE

  • Mandatory age confirmation at signup, with account suspension for any account later found to misrepresent age.
  • Profile and content moderation, including image review for uploaded photos.
  • In-app reporting and blocking tools available on every profile, chat, and piece of content.
  • Proactive account review for behavior patterns associated with grooming or exploitation.
  • Removal of any account, message, or media found to contain or facilitate CSAE, without delay.

How to Report a Concern

If you encounter content or behavior on Let's Meet that concerns you regarding child safety, please report it right away using any of the following methods:

  1. In-app report: Tap the report icon on any profile, message, or piece of content and select the relevant concern category.
  2. Email: Send details, screenshots, and the reported user's profile information to our safety team (contact below).
  3. Immediate danger: If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local law enforcement or emergency services first, then report to us.

Our Response Process

Every CSAE-related report is treated as high priority. Once received, we:

  • Immediately suspend the reported account pending review.
  • Preserve relevant account data and content as evidence.
  • Escalate confirmed cases to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via their CyberTipline, and to local law enforcement as required by applicable law.
  • Permanently ban confirmed violators from the platform.

Legal Compliance

Let's Meet complies with all applicable child protection laws, including reporting obligations under the laws of Nigeria and, where applicable to our users and hosting providers, U.S. federal law (18 U.S.C. §2258A) regarding mandatory reporting of CSAE to NCMEC.
